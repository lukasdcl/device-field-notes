# Apple AirPort Extreme (6th gen, 802.11ac, 2013)

The tall white tower model, model number A1521.

## Power

**Broadcasting Wi-Fi:** 8 W.

**Wi-Fi turned off, connected over Ethernet only:** 5.3 W.

Switching the Wi-Fi radios off saves about 2.7 W, roughly a third of its draw. Most of the power goes on keeping the box itself running, not on the Wi-Fi.

**How I measured:** a power-monitoring smart plug at the wall socket. That includes everything the router draws from the mains, including its built-in power supply.
