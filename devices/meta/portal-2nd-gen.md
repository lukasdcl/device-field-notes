# Meta Portal (2nd gen, 10-inch, 2021)

## Power

**Powered on, screen off:** 1.0–1.2 W most of the time. Once a minute it spikes to about 2.5–3 W for around 5 seconds, then drops back.

**Screen on, running the IMMORTAL screensaver:** usually 1.6 W, spiking to about 3.5 W once a minute.

**Caveat:** Wi-Fi was on and connected, but the device's internet access was blocked, so it could only reach the local network. With full internet access, figures may differ: it would be free to fetch updates and content.
