# De'Longhi Capsule HFX30C18.IW ceramic fan heater

Rated by De'Longhi at 1800 W (high) and 900 W (low).

## Power

With the thermostat turned to maximum:

| Setting | Measured | Rated |
|---|---|---|
| 2 (high) | 1168 W | 1800 W |
| 1 (low) | 679 W | 900 W |
| Fan only | 6.5 W | |
| Off | 0.00 W | |

Both heat settings were steady, varying by a few watts at most. Readings were taken about 10 minutes after switching on.

**Measured draw is well below the rating:** about 65% of it on high and 75% on low. That's typical of ceramic heaters. Their heating elements are made of a material whose electrical resistance rises as it heats up, so they draw the most power from cold and settle lower once warm. The rating is closer to that cold-start figure than to what the heater draws once it's running.

Off really is off: no standby draw at all.

**How I measured:** an IKEA GRILLPLATS smart plug at the wall socket, reading its power figure.
