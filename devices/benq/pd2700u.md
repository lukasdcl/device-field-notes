# BenQ PD2700U

27-inch 4K monitor. BenQ rates it at 24 W in normal use, 80 W maximum and under 0.5 W in sleep mode.

## Power

| State | Measured |
|---|---|
| In normal use | 22–25 W |
| Switching on | brief spike to 30 W |
| Connected computer asleep | 0.4 W, but occasionally around 20 W |
| Switched off with its power button | 0.0–0.2 W |

Normal use matches BenQ's 24 W figure closely. The 80 W maximum presumably covers things like full brightness, the built-in speakers and charging devices from its USB ports at the same time.

**The odd one:** with the computer asleep it usually drops to 0.4 W, but I've occasionally seen it drawing around 20 W, close to its in-use figure, while the computer was still asleep. I haven't pinned down why. Possible causes include the computer briefly waking in the background (which sends a video signal again) or the monitor waking to search its inputs. If you're relying on sleep to save power, switching it off with the button is the only state that's reliably low.
